Our Story

From One Employee to a Skilled Team

WestPro Construction began as a family-owned business with a single committed employee and a simple promise: do exceptional work and treat every client with honesty.

That promise resonated. Today we've grown into a robust team of more than ten employees and over twenty-five skilled professionals, each an expert in their respective role. As we've grown, our standards have only sharpened — we continue the legacy of integrity and excellence that began with City Construction, delivering results that exceed expectations on every project.

We're proud to call Snohomish, WA home, and prouder still to help our neighbors protect and beautify the places they live and work.
