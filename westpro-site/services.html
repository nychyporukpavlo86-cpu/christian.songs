Enhance Your Property Today

As a trusted exterior contractor, we handle every layer of your building's shell — siding, masonry, roofing and more — with durable, name-brand materials and skilled craftsmanship.

Siding

Protect and transform your exterior with expertly installed siding. We specialize in James Hardie fiber cement — plus cedar, wood and metal options — chosen to fit your home's look and the Pacific Northwest climate.

  • James Hardie fiber cement siding
  • Cedar & natural wood siding
  • Metal siding
  • Repairs & full replacement

Masonry

Add strength, texture and timeless curb appeal with quality masonry. From stone and brick veneer to coping and custom accents, our masons deliver clean, durable work that lasts.

  • Stone & brick veneer
  • Coping & caps
  • Decorative accents
  • Repairs & restoration

Roofing

Your roof is your first line of defense. Our experienced crews install and repair residential roofing built to keep the weather out — and every qualifying install is covered by our workmanship warranty.

  • Roof installation & replacement
  • Roof repairs
  • Gutter installation
  • Lifetime workmanship warranty

Decks

Extend your living space with a custom deck designed for how you actually use your yard. We build and restore decks, railings and stairs that stand up to the seasons.

  • Custom deck design & build
  • Railings & stairs
  • Deck repairs & restoration

Fencing

Define your property with fencing that balances privacy, security and curb appeal. Cleanly built, straight lines and durable materials for years of reliable service.

  • Wood & privacy fencing
  • Property-line fencing
  • Gates & access points

Windows & Doors

Refresh your facade and improve efficiency with new windows and doors. Proper installation and weather sealing keep drafts out and comfort in — with warranty-backed workmanship.

  • Window replacement
  • Entry & patio doors
  • Weather sealing & trim
